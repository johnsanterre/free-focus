AI for Nonprofits

Your staff are already using AI, in grant drafts, donor emails and meeting notes. Free Focus helps you put simple rules around it, keep donor data safe, and get real value from it, at no cost to 501(c)(3) organizations.

Your AI policy, written with you in one free hour

Templates are easy to find. Turning one into rules your team will actually follow is the hard part. In a single working session we write your policy with you: what data may go into AI tools, who checks AI output before it goes out, which accounts staff use, and how you tell donors and partners about your AI use. You leave with a finished document, not a homework assignment. The session is free for qualified 501(c)(3) nonprofits.

What else we offer

Keeping donor data safe

  • Use your organization's work accounts for AI tools (Microsoft Copilot or Google Gemini signed in with your work login), so your data is not used to train public models.
  • Never paste donor names, giving history, health details or passwords into a free public chatbot.
  • Treat every AI answer as a draft: check facts, figures and quotes before anything leaves the building.

Live sessions with an AI expert

Short, practical sessions for staff and boards, taught by a PhD in artificial intelligence who has built AI systems in industry and teaches machine learning at the graduate level.

  • How AI tools actually work, and where they fail
  • Getting good results from AI in everyday work
  • Measuring your programs with the data you already have

Hands-on walkthroughs
